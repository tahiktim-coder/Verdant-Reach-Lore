# Still Water

Still Water is the fishing-horror sister project, set in the same universe as the Verdant Reach: a lone fisherman, a glass-still lake, a golden fish that grants wishes word for word, and a red sun that is not a sun. The premise is yours ([CANON]); nearly all lines are the game's script ([GAME TEXT]) co-written in the studio, or the studio's story map ([STUDIO PROPOSAL]). Related: [16-kolobok.md](16-kolobok.md), [08-characters-and-stories.md](08-characters-and-stories.md), [07-creatures-and-peoples.md](07-creatures-and-peoples.md), [06-places.md](06-places.md).

## Premise

- **[CANON]** (studio lore) A fishing-horror pixel game. You catch a talking fish that grants wishes. Then the sun sets and a menacing red sun rises.
- **[CANON]** (studio lore) Same universe as the Verdant Reach. The art style comes from your @nakofrish image titled “AM: The Verdant Reach”. (“AM” here is only that image's title. The rejected “Continent of AM” in [12-archive.md](12-archive.md) stays rejected.) The boat scene is the quality bar for every studio visual.
- **[GAME TEXT]** The title screen calls it “A short fishing tale”. Controls: “Tap to cast. Tap when the float dips. Hold to reel.” Prompts: “Tap to cast”, “Wait for the float to dip”, “Tap now!”, “Hold to reel. Let go when it pulls hard.”
- **[GAME TEXT]** Fishing-failure captions: “The line snapped.” (tension at maximum), “It slipped away. Cast again.” (missed golden bite), “It slipped the hook.” (missed ordinary bite), “Too early. Nothing was biting yet.” and the golden variant “Too early.”
- **[GAME TEXT]** (from Reach Field Tips, card 3) “If the fish offers a wish, say it word for word. When it asks for your last one, cut the line.” The card shows the lake under the red sun with a boat, a fisher in dark silhouette, a line and rings on the water. The Reach-themed deck includes the lake, but no Reach traveller is drawn on it.
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** Pebble folk live on this lake's shore, and the old woman's wish that made the loaf came from this fish. See [16-kolobok.md](16-kolobok.md).

## The lake

- **[GAME TEXT]** Opening caption: “The lake is so still it could be glass.” A mountain lake: a tall peak on the right, a ridge on the left, far ridges at the horizon, birds by day, fish jumping until the sky turns red.
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** (story map, draft 7) Opening: the boat rows in at dawn. “Nothing on the lake is moving except you.” The fisherman's tapped thoughts: “Something interesting, for once.” “Look at that sun.” “I could stay out here forever.” The fish later quotes every one of these back.
- **[GAME TEXT]** Captions as the fish nears, one per act: “The water goes very still.” “Something gold turns beneath the surface.” “The lantern flame leans toward the water.” Story map: “The water goes very still again.”
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** (story map) Act 2 caption variant: “The water goes very still. The lantern flame leans toward it.” Act 2 beat: “once, the lantern gutters and the water is full of eyes”.

## The stranger's bait

- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** (story map) “The stranger’s bait. Cursed or blessed, he said. It has an eye.” The stranger is never seen. At the end the fish admits “That bait was never for fish. I should have said.”

## The golden fish

- **[CANON]** (studio lore) A talking fish that grants wishes. **[GAME TEXT]** Muster lines: “One wish. Choose the words.” “I grant it word for word.” “When I ask for your last wish, cut the line.” Muster lore line: “Grants wishes word for word. Then the sun goes red.”
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** Design rule: the fish never lies and never takes. It gives what you literally asked for and reads your words back as consent. After every grant it repeats your option label (“A home on the shore. Done.”). At the last wish it recounts everything you got, always ending with the sun: “Everything you asked for. Someone to sit with you. A boat full of gold. And forever. Your words, not mine.” “There it is. Forever, like you said.”
- **[GAME TEXT]** First words: “Wait. Don’t gut me, fisherman.” “Put me back, and I’ll grant you a wish. Three, if you’re patient.” Let it go: “Kind. Kindness is rare out here.” Keep it: “You tighten your grip. It slides through your fingers like water.” “Cold hands can’t hold me. Now. A wish.” Then “Tell me what you want.” Story-map variants: “Something interesting, you said. Here I am.” “First time here, you said. Nobody comes here twice.” “Kind. Nobody kind comes out this far alone.” Kept: the fish lies in the boat and dims each act, “Cold hands. He had cold hands too.” “Keep me, then. The wish comes anyway.” Later: “You cast anyway. Habit.” and, when the lake keeps count, “The fish in the boat does not.”
- **[GAME TEXT]** It is named “Golden fish” by day and night, and “The fish” in the red. Drawn with a crowned dorsal fin, forked tail, barbel and pale eye. In the Muster it is a huge head breaking the surface with a big pale eye, pupil on the viewer, rimmed from behind by the red sun.
- **[GAME TEXT]** Mechanic: when the golden fish is next, the float is drawn gold and sparkles, its bite window is longer (3.2 s against 0.95 s) and it never snaps the line. The golden fish surfaces beside the boat with a splash, rings and a chime, and bobs and sparkles while visible.
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** Endgame confession: “I sat where you sit. I said what you said. Three times.” “I’d like to go home now.” Kept-fish whisper at the last wish: “I’m sorry. I want to go home too.” **[PROPOSED FIX]** This bible's reading, which the story map implies but never states: the fish was once a fisherman. **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** Home ending note: “Next summer the golden fish speaks in your voice.”

## The three wishes

- **[GAME TEXT]** (built) Wish 1: “Someone to sit with me” / “More fish” / “A home on the shore”. Wish 2: “Make this day last forever” / “Let me understand the fish” / “Gold. A boat full of it”. Wish 3: “Let me go home” / “Take the red sun away” / “Cut the line”.
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** (story map) Labels there read “Let me hear the fish” and “Take the light away”. It adds “Nothing” at wishes 1 and 2, swaps “More fish” for “Take me where the fish are”, and adds earned third options “Stay with them”, “Let me get my gold” and “Nothing”.
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** (story map) Wish-1 prompt: “What would you like, fisherman?” The built prompt is “Tell me what you want.”
- **[GAME TEXT]** Company: “Done. Someone will keep you company.” “Don’t ask them who they are.” More fish: “Done. This lake will never be empty again.” Home: “Done. Look, a light on the shore.” “It was always yours. You just never noticed.” Then: “When you want the next wish, just cast.” and the caption “The sun sits a little lower now.”
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** Cost rule: “A wish costs a little daylight. You said you could stay out here forever.” “You’ll get to.” The sun drops as it speaks. Nothing costs nothing: “Nobody asks for nothing. I’ll ask again.” “The lake stays glass.” Later: “You again. Still wanting nothing?” and, if you then ask for something, “You asked once for nothing. It remembered.”
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** (story map) The second refusal in full: “Twice.” “Nobody asks for nothing twice. What are you?”
- **[GAME TEXT]** Act 1 greeting: “Back so soon? The lake remembers every wish.” “Your second?” (Story map: “Back so soon? The lake keeps count.” “And this time?”) Forever: “Forever is a long time for a sun.” “Let this one rest. I know a sun that never sets.” Understand: “Listen, then.” “They talk about me constantly.” Gold: “Gold is heavy. Mind the waterline.” Closing: “Watch.” or “Look how late it’s gotten.”
- **[GAME TEXT]** (built) The gold wish puts a gold pile in the boat and the boat sinks two pixels. The “More fish” wish spawns sixteen fish shadows under the water and bites come faster.
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** Forever: “This one is tired. I know one that never sets.” Clouds, birds and the sun freeze, and no stars come up. Gold sinks the boat under him; he swims, the lantern floats: “Sorry. Gold is heavy. You can always come back for it.” First refusal at wish 2: “Full already? It’s a little late for that.” Second: “Twice. What are you?” Wish-3 callbacks: “You wanted a sun that never sets. It never will.” “That light on the shore. Who did you think lit it?” “The gold came up from the bottom. So did I.”

## The companion

- **[GAME TEXT]** A dark hooded silhouette who fades onto the plank behind the fisherman, facing away. In the red he turns (the palette keeps a slot for his red eyes) and whispers “Don’t answer it. Cut the line.”
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** “Who?” “Doesn’t matter. Someone.” “Someone. Nobody asks who.” “If they ask you anything, don’t answer.” Tapped, he repeats your opening words. After the lantern he asks, unprompted, “Will you stay?” with the question mark drawn wrong. Say Yes and “your one word joins the charge sheet” (“‘Yes.’ Your word.”); in the red he says “You said you’d stay.” Stay silent and his whisper reads as a friend's. Across runs: “First time here. You said that last time.” “Look at that sun. Still there.” “We could stay out here forever.” Char card: “Its scales show someone sitting behind you.”
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** (story map) Yes to the companion: “He does not react. The question mark fades” and “The recount at wish 3 gains ‘someone who will stay’”.

## The cabin

- **[GAME TEXT]** A small dark house with a lit window on the far shore, under the sun. In the red: “The light on the shore was mine too. It always is.”
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** “A home on the shore. One has just come free.” “Every light out here is for someone. That one is for you.” Grayling card: “It smells of woodsmoke. Of the cabin.” Endings: the light goes out, “Nobody was inside” (Home); it stays on, “Nobody comes down” (Dark); the window goes dark at dawn (Still water).

## The ocean

- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** Wish “Take me where the fish are”: the mountains sink into haze, the boat shrinks to a speck on an ocean, huge shadows pass, one settles under you. “Where the fish are. I’d leave the big one. It’s been waiting longer than you have.” Cast now and you are Swallowed. Wait about ten seconds and it slides away; the shore never comes back and the run continues on open sea. “Look how they all go the same way.” Trout card: “Its stomach is full of hooks. It kept biting.” At the end: “All those fish. Did you see which way they swam?”
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** (story map) Ocean wish, Still water ending: “the shadows follow the line down”.
- **[GAME TEXT]** (built) The simpler “More fish” wish spawns fish shadows that, once the sky goes red, all steer toward one point near the boat. “All those fish I gave you. Did you think they were food?”

## The sunset and the red sun that is not a sun

- **[CANON]** (studio lore) After the wishes the sun sets and a menacing red sun rises.
- **[GAME TEXT]** “The sun slips into the lake like a coin into a well.” “You light the lantern.” Story map adds: “That was my second, too. This one costs the rest of the day.” “You’ll miss the sun. I’ll bring you another.”
- **[GAME TEXT]** Red sequence: “The line goes taut. Something is pulling it toward the horizon.” (Story map: “The line goes taut. You did not feel a bite.”) The fish surfaces where the sun set. “One wish left.” “But first, the sun I promised you.” “Something rises where the sun went down.” It is twice the real sun's size, hangs on a line (a stalk) from above the sky, opens a narrow pupil, shifts it toward the boat, and a heartbeat sounds. Ash drifts down. “It is not a sun.”
- **[GAME TEXT]** The fish: “Every sun is bait, fisherman. Look up. Do you see the line?” “You thought you were fishing. You were swimming toward the light.” “And those were never mountains.” Per wish: “You wanted a sun that never sets. Here it is.” “And all that gold. You’ll sink so nicely.” “Make your last wish.”
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** “You said forever. I listened.” Kept: “I’m right here, fisherman.” / “That isn’t me pulling. It never was.” Two refusals: the pupil never follows the boat, “You gave me nothing. It wants to see why.”
- **[PROPOSED FIX]** The red sun is a thing that fishes for people with suns as bait; the lake is its line. Do not connect it to the sun-as-prison cosmology in your notebook without deciding to.

## The voices on the water

- **[GAME TEXT]** After wishing to understand the fish, each later catch carries a voice: “When it asks for your last wish, cut the line.” “That sun is bait.” “Don’t look at the horizon.” At the last wish “The lake” whispers “cut the line cut the line cut the line”.
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** “They all say the same thing.” Voice keyed to your first wish: “I wished for company too. Now I have plenty.” or “I wanted nothing too. It waited.” The lake whispers “That sun is bait.” as the line appears. The voices read as earlier fishermen; the story map implies this rather than saying it.

## Every ending and its meaning

- **[GAME TEXT]** **Home** (“Let me go home”): “Home. Yes.” “Come inside.” The sky and mountains slide down as a jaw, fangs along the horizon, then black. Text: “The lake is quiet again. The fish are hungry. Somewhere, a new sun is rising for the next fisherman.” **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** Meaning: you go inside the moment and it closes; next summer the golden fish speaks in your voice.
- **[GAME TEXT]** **Dark** (“Take the red sun away”): “As you wish.” “Without light, you won’t see the teeth. That is my kindness.” An eyelid closes over the red sun, the lantern dies. Text: “You sit with the lantern until it gutters out. Sometimes something takes the bait. You never reel it in.” **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** Meaning: you refuse to look and it passes over you; you never leave. Once, the water is full of eyes. If you let the fish go, it circles the boat all night.
- **[GAME TEXT]** **Still water** (“Cut the line”): “You draw your knife across the line.” The fish: “No. Nobody cuts the” and is cut off. Caption “You cut the line.” The stalk falls, the red sun sinks hissing, a real dawn, you row away. Text: “You row until the water is only water. You never fish here again. Some evenings, the sunset looks back.” **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** Meaning: you let go and keep the wound. The gold goes over, the window goes dark, the stranger stays in the stern: “There is someone in the stern. You do not ask. You row.” Kept fish: at dawn you lift it over the side.
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** **Stay** (earned by answering the companion): the boat drifts to the shore light, he turns to face you at last, no sunrise. The soft Home: you chose the good day over the morning.
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** **Deep** (earned with gold): the horizon rises past the screen, the reflection becomes the sky, no bottom, stars below. “The gold is where you left it. So is everything else.” A kept fish goes down with you.
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** **Swallowed** (cast while the big one is under you): a circle of dark water opens and takes the water with it. A ninety-second run.
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** **Silent** (Nothing three times): the line goes slack, a tail splash, dawn. “You wanted nothing. It had nothing to show you.” Counts as Still water.
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** Per-wish ending captions: company “The seat beside you is empty now. It was your turn.” / “Someone breathes behind you all night.”; forever “The day does not end. You aren’t in it.” / “It never begins either.” / “Dawn comes anyway. You had forgotten it could.”; hear “You know the words already. You will say them.” / “The lake keeps talking. You stop answering.” / “You can still hear them from the shore.”; gold “The gold goes down first. It has done this before.” / “The boat rides low. You do not bail.”; ocean “The lake is full. It was always full.” / “Something is always biting. You let them.”; kept “Thank you.” / “Don’t leave me in the boat.”; let go “Something gold sinks past you. Free.”
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** The story map names Gogol's Viy as the rule behind Dark and Pushkin's last page behind Silent. Private touchstones so far; see the writing guide's rule on recognizable signatures and decide whether they stay.

## The cross-run lines

- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** After any ending the title shows a tiny far boat: the next fisherman. Fish: “You again. Or someone wearing you.” After Home: “Back out already? It doesn’t usually let go.” First card: “There is an old hook in its lip.” Nothing is locked behind replay; these are four lines.

## The flags

- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** Eight small facts; nothing forks the world, the flags recolour it: **kept**, **wishes[]**, **firstAsk**, **refused** (0 to 2), **answered**, **heard**, **gold**, **endings (saved)**. Rule: callbacks, not forks; a choice matters when a later line proves the game saw it. Writing rule: no line says memory, past, stuck, or lure; bait is said once. Runtime 4.5 to 6.5 minutes; the earned endings replace the last minute.
