## What the images say about the world's look

These are mood notes, not a style to copy exactly. The wooden caption signs are only the TikTok post format and are not part of the world.

- **Atmosphere:** old, lived-in, softly lit. Golden-hour amber and dusk teal show up most. Big skies and dramatic clouds.
- **Scale contrast:** small figures against enormous things such as wyrms, storms, phoenixes, treefolk, clockwork towers and floating isles. This matches the monumental-versus-ordinary tone in [13-writing-guide.md](13-writing-guide.md).
- **Landscapes:** alpine lakes with castles on the shore (12, 28), forest roads, cliff edges looking over wide valleys, sea cliffs and coves.
- **Architecture:** Gothic castles with tall spires, ancient stone arches, aqueduct bridges (36), and clockwork towers with giant gears (35, 38).
- **Knights:** worn plate armour, red capes very common, closed helms. The blonde companion appears often (02, 16, 19).
- **Cozy moments:** a café/kitchen with knights (01), a roadside shop (06), snacks traded on the road (33).

## A second visual reference set: the studio pieces

The Reach Studio (`studio/`) holds code-drawn pixel pieces that are a second reference set beside the 43 images. Unlike the TikTok images, their lore files are marked [his], [working] and [proposed], so what is marked [his] ranks with dedicated lore. Their player-facing text is **[GAME TEXT]**. The quality bar is the Still Water boat scene. You loved the floured loaf in the sunset valley and the Muster creature menu, and liked the Summit Road's lighting but not the rest of it.

- **Still Water** (fishing horror, red-sun ramp): a lone rowing boat on a glass lake among mountains, one light in frame. Live: https://claude.ai/artifact/FPAPfmQk1pJAg8wAp7Q4gW
- **Kolobok** (playable): the floured loaf rolling through a dusk wheat valley, a stone elder and pebble folk on the hilltop.
- **Reachbound: Muster of the Reach** (9 portraits): Pebble Folk, Loaf Crows, Stone Elder, Verdant Knight, The Collector, The Fish, The Projection, The Covenant's Hunter, Kolobok. Live: https://claude.ai/artifact/XKmtZuNeVjrmTn7FtQZ2Ex
- **Reach Field Tips** (13 cards, catalog below). Live: https://claude.ai/artifact/Kry5ZuNr7mC5zvnZXmZPTG
- **Hero of the Reach**: a Verdant Knight's character sheet, green tabard with a gold leaf, candlelit stone room, the Reach at dusk through an arched window. Its stats, skills and the name Sir Aldren are studio placeholders.
- **The Summit Road**: the cursed warrior walking the endless mountain of Old magic. Lighting liked; the rest not.
- The Souls-games studies (tower, city, wolf) are not Reach lore and fell short of the bar in your eyes.

Look notes from the pieces: one hue-shifting colour ramp per scene, the light source always in frame (sun, moon, lantern, rift, circle), empty space and one focal subject, hand-drawn silhouettes. Palettes: dusk (Kolobok, Stone Elder), night lake (Pebble Folk), green dawn (Verdant Knight), red sun (Still Water, the Fish), storm violet (the Hunter). Mint-green glowing eyes on the stone peoples and the things that watch are a studio art convention, not lore. The wooden plank signs on the Field Tips are the card format, like the TikTok caption signs, not part of the world.
