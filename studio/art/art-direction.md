# Art direction

## The bar
- **Still Water's boat scene** is the reference for quality: clean, calm, one light, one subject.
- His remakes of the "Souls games pixel-art" posts (tower, city, wolf; `examples/souls/`) fell short of it in his eyes.
  He chose rebuilding visuals in code over animating his original images.
- Pixel work should feel **majestic, medieval-fantasy**; this world leans horror-tinged and folkloric.

## What he liked and didn't
- Kept from the style experiments: **Kabbalistic minimalism** (good for concepts), **VHS broadcast** ("super great"),
  **declassified dossier**.
- Hated: the **hand-drawn ink sketch** look.
- Loved: Kolobok (the floured loaf in the sunset valley) and the Muster creature menu.
- Everything should be usable in real games (itch.io, Steam) and in his TikTok videos (text overlays for editing).

## Rules
The rules and the workflow are in `.claude/skills/pixel-scenes/SKILL.md`. Don't restate them; follow them.
