# Still Water

## His concept [his]
- A fishing-horror pixel game. You catch a talking fish that grants wishes. Then the sun sets and a menacing red sun rises.
- Art style taken from his @nakofrish image "AM: The Verdant Reach".
- Same universe as the Verdant Reach.
- A playable prototype exists (`.claude/skills/pixel-scenes/examples/still-water/`). Its boat scene is the quality bar
  for every visual in this repo.

## In the prototype [lines co-written with Claude; check with him]
- Voices on the water: "When it asks for your last wish, cut the line." "That sun is bait." "Don't look at the horizon."
- Endings: Home, Dark, Still water.
- The Muster menu gives the fish these lines: "One wish. Choose the words." "I grant it word for word."

## Links to other lore [proposed]
- In the Kolobok proposal, the old woman's wish came from this fish, and pebble folk live on this lake's shore.
