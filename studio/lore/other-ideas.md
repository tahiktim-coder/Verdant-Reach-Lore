# Other ideas from his notebook [his]

Not placed in the Verdant Reach universe yet. Ask before connecting them.

- **Relic Vine:** a magical fruit species long extinct; all that remains is an ancient living vine (like centuries-old
  real grapevines) carrying an echo of the vanished fruit. Connects to a **sun-as-prison cosmology**: a god sacrificed
  his human form to permanently trap a corrupt peer-tier entity, and the frozen result is visible daily in the sky.
- **Speech-delay elixir** magic system.
- **Visual concept:** a kneeling knight in near-monochrome, kabbalistic minimalist indie style, grabs sand; his fist
  opens like a shell and releases a small sand golem that runs away. The golem may be the player character.
- **Characters from real stories:** adapt viral real-life hero and survivor stories into his fiction.
- **Design interests:** emergent systems (RimWorld, Bannerlord), geopolitical game theory, mythological and occult
  sources (Kabbalah, Enochian magic, Nordic mythology) as research.
- **A separate 2040 sci-fi setting:** "The Loom" (62-reactor complex, SENA quasi-AGI) and dimensional threshold zones.
