# Underfoot: the Lantern Giant

Underfoot is the fourth sister project: a small browser stealth game about hiding in a walled hill town from a giant nearly three kilometres tall that comes each night with a lantern on a chain and takes what its light finds. The game is built and playable (https://tahiktim-coder.github.io/underfoot/, source at github.com/tahiktim-coder/underfoot). Its text and its link to the Reach are proposals from the Underfoot session of 2026-10-29; nothing here is canon until you mark it.

Related files: [07-creatures-and-peoples.md](07-creatures-and-peoples.md) (Giants), [03-knights-and-orders.md](03-knights-and-orders.md) (Verdant Knights), [01-the-reach.md](01-the-reach.md) (the Elder Frontiers), [17-studio-and-games.md](17-studio-and-games.md).

## Premise

- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** The town is a walled hill town on an Elder Frontier of the Reach (the landscape class of ancient or enormous forces, [01-the-reach.md](01-the-reach.md)). The giant is one of the ancient giants ([07-creatures-and-peoples.md](07-creatures-and-peoples.md), "what the giants are is open"): this one wears plate of old iron, a great helm whose face is never seen whole (it stands in cloud from the shoulders up), and carries a lantern on a chain the length of a valley. It walks down from the north each night, searches the streets with the light, takes whoever the light holds, and goes north again by morning.
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** The player is a Verdant Knight (the red cape; [03-knights-and-orders.md](03-knights-and-orders.md): educated, a guardian, a keeper of old knowledge), alone in the town for seven nights. The knight's job in the game is only to stay out of the light; the knight's record on the night cards is the lore voice.
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** Where it goes: north, to the Cavern of Giants (the Cavern is canon; that this giant returns there is not).
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** What it is, in the game's own words, is never said. Open questions kept open on purpose: who armoured it, whether it judges or merely collects, whether the town is the last of several.

## Game text

- **[GAME TEXT]** Title: "UNDERFOOT" / "Something is walking the valley." / "It takes what its light finds." / "Stay in the dark." / "Seven nights to dawn."
- **[GAME TEXT]** The HUD's one label for it: "Quiet", "It is coming", "It is here", "It is looking", "It is giving up", "It sees you", "It is passing", "Dawn". Over the knight: "Hidden", "Half seen", "Seen".
- **[GAME TEXT]** The knight's record, one line on each night's card:
  1. "It came from the north, lantern first."
  2. "The old maps call this the Elder Frontier."
  3. "No order remembers who armoured it."
  4. "It does not take the ones who stay dark."
  5. "By morning it is always gone north again."
  6. "One more night."
- **[GAME TEXT]** The dawn card, after the seventh night: "Dawn." / "Seven nights. It has gone north, to the Cavern of Giants." / the score.
- **[GAME TEXT]** The over card: "It found you." / "Night N. You lasted S s, your best B s." / one of "Caught in the open.", "Caught in the half light.", "You stayed too long under it.", "It chased you down." / the score.

## What the game settles and what it leaves open

- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** Settled by play: the giant's light is the only thing that finds you; deep shadow is safe, the edge of the light is not; it follows your footsteps a few seconds behind and comes along your path once it has seen you; the townspeople it takes burn where they stand and leave a stain.
- **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** Left open: everything about the giant's nature, the order that made its armour, why this town, whether the knight was sent.
