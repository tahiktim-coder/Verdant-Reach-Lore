# Open Questions

These are deliberately unresolved. **Don't invent answers casually.** Some should stay mysteries, because the Reach has always worked better when not everything is explained. Mark the ones you want to answer.

## Verdant politics
- Why are there castles but no countries? What binds the castle domains together?
- Is there any central council? Who governs the Reach overall?
- Who settles conflicts between castles?
- Are castle domains inherited?
- How does Verdant nobility work?
- How are major laws enforced?
- How old is the Reach? Traditions go back at least about a thousand years, but its true history seems far older.
- Is the whole Reach the "western plain", or is the plain one region of a larger, mostly alpine Reach? If the latter, where does the wheat country of the games lie, and what is it called? Does the studio's "west" replace the bible's "southwest", or is the alpine heartland the southwestern part of a western Reach (see S1, S7)?
- Does the Reach's own far north (the Colossal Starblooms) lie before the mountain path, or beyond it?
- Do road wardens exist as an office of the castle domains (studio proposal), and who pays them? Who are the wardens who hang flour bells: knights, castle servants, a guild, or villagers?
