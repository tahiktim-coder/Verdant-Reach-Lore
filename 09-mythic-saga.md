# The Mythic Saga

Separate from the grounded geopolitical lore, you built a highly visual myth-history that follows the red-caped knight across enormous ages. It is more symbolic than normal chronology. Treat it as a **mythic branch** until its relation to the grounded story is decided. It could be literal history, myth, several knights merged into one legend, symbolic history, an immortal figure, reincarnation, or time distortion. Don't assume the knight literally lived through every era. See [10-contradictions-and-fixes.md](10-contradictions-and-fixes.md).

**Studio lore note.** The studio notes add a second, plainer spine of ages (the god-war era, the Old Age, the New Age, then now) and three legends with their own protagonists: the cursed warrior of the Old Age, the reincarnating Librarians, and the New Age necromancer fought by Verdant Reach knights. None of them is the red-caped knight. The cursed warrior is a king's son and is never called a knight. Keep them apart unless you decide otherwise. The legends are in [05-ancient-history.md](05-ancient-history.md); how the eras may line up with this saga is below.

## Branch A (earlier): two knights

**[PROVISIONAL]** This branch follows two knights, not one. It was later restarted, but it hasn't been discarded.

### 1. The Age of Beasts
The world is ruled by enormous primordial creatures: dragons, titans, forest spirits, colossal living relics, sleeping forest giants, gigantic serpents, huge tortoises, leviathans and phoenixes.
Scenes accepted later: phoenixes rising from seas of cloud, crystal-backed beasts, bridges made of dragon ribs, serpents moving under grass, living canyon murals, waterfalls flowing upward, inverted creatures, living constellations.

### 2. Abyssal Depths
The knights descend into something like a primordial ocean, or the memory of the world. Imagery: translucent leviathans, gates of coral and bone, skeleton whales, bone reefs, drowned temples, giant jellyfish, cathedrals made of titan bones, symbolic ocean vortices.

### 3. Fall of Titans
The giant creatures fight, and the world itself becomes collateral damage. Imagery: sky serpents, leviathans, collapsing mountains, boiling oceans, glowing giant corpses, flame titans, an ocean leviathan against a sky dragon, glass wastelands, a blood-red eclipse.
This branch was moving toward the Age of Artifice.

## Branch B (later, main): one red-caped knight

**[PROVISIONAL]** You restarted with a single red-caped knight and accepted this sequence:

### 1. The First Light
The mythic beginning: an earlier world before organized civilization dominates. Its cosmology isn't defined.

### 2. The First Kingdoms
Civilization emerges. Motifs: fire, steel, fortresses, cities, kingdoms, war. Human political order starts reshaping the primordial world.

### 3. The Rebellion of Ash
The knight joins a rebellion against an empire or power. Themes: war, empire, defiance, war machines, loss. This is the shift from heroic civilization to industrialized conflict. Who the rebellion fought is open.

### 4. The Age of Artifice
Machines reshape civilization: brass cities, gigantic forges, mechanical infrastructure, artificial beings, mechanical titans. Technology becomes a force that can create the next age.

### 5. The Voice of Iron
Machines awaken. Some hold or inherit the minds and voices of fallen gods. The machines know the red-caped knight and start speaking his name. Machinery becomes mythology.

### 6. The Storm of Saints
The machines develop religion and begin worshipping the knight or his myth. One title for him: **First Flame**. Technological civilization turns memory into faith.

### 7. The Hollow God
A false or artificial god is made in the knight's image. The real person and the civilization's myth of him are no longer the same thing. Human and machine faith manufactures something divine but empty. Who built it is open.

### 8 / 9. The Memory Eclipse and The Age of Quiet Wonder
Both were accepted. Their order relative to each other was never settled.

**The Memory Eclipse.** A black, eclipsed sun makes memory visible. Forgotten battles appear around people, along with lost names and faces, other versions of a life, forgotten companions, lives that may never have happened, another self, recognition and grief, and memory physically burning through the world. It builds toward the act of remembering.

**The Age of Quiet Wonder.** Accepted more than once. An epilogue: after gods, titans, empires and machines, civilization reaches an age where the extraordinary becomes gentle. Themes: rebuilding, exploration, rediscovering beauty, ordinary people living among ancient wonders, peaceful adventures, and the knight becoming more myth than warrior. It reconnects the mythic history with the cozy, travelling side of the Reach.

**[PROPOSED FIX]** Order: Hollow God → Memory Eclipse → Age of Quiet Wonder. The Eclipse resolves the Hollow God by making the world remember the real man behind the false god. That remembering is what allows the gentle age that follows. Quiet Wonder works best as the final note.

## The studio's three eras against this saga

- **[CANON]** (studio lore) Your era scheme runs god-war era, Old Age, New Age, then the present; the definitions are in [05-ancient-history.md](05-ancient-history.md).
- **[PROVISIONAL]** The saga above and the era scheme are both yours. They do not contradict each other outright, but they are two chronologies and the bible needs to know how they sit together. See the era-mapping contradiction in [10-contradictions-and-fixes.md](10-contradictions-and-fixes.md).
- **[PROPOSED FIX]** One mapping that keeps the strongest parts of both:

| Studio era | Saga ages | Why |
|---|---|---|
| God-war era | The First Light; Branch A (Age of Beasts, Abyssal Depths, Fall of Titans) | "Mythological-tier beings fought" is the Fall of Titans, where giant creatures fight and the world is collateral damage. The "gods" godlike to humans are the titans and primordial beasts. The war of gods left colossal blades. An ancient mage made the rally scroll to bring allies to allies fighting gods, so mortals were already in it. |
| Old Age | The First Kingdoms, the Rebellion of Ash, the Age of Artifice | Kings and realms, archmages, warriors with channeling gear, Librarians preserving magic and technology, the Library vanishing. Late-stage ancient magic plus Engineers who build constructs read as the age when artifice peaks. On this reading the arcane-mech civilization is an Old Age power. |
| New Age | The Voice of Iron, the Storm of Saints, the Hollow God, the Memory Eclipse | Machines inherit the voices of fallen gods (gods that fell in the god-war era). The mages overthrow the arcane-mech civilization. Verdant Reach knights of some castle fight the necromancer. "Also ancient now." |
| Present | The Age of Quiet Wonder | Ordinary people among ancient wonders: the covenant knights, Kolobok on the roads, Still Water. |

- **[PROPOSED FIX]** Alternative: keep the two schemes separate. The era scheme is how Reach scholars divide time; the saga is mythic memory and does not need one-to-one dates. This is the safer default if you do not want to lock the saga to history.
- **[PROPOSED FIX]** On either reading, the "gods" of the Voice of Iron, the Hollow God and the Valley of Forgotten Gods should be read the way your notes define them: godlike to humans, not universe creators.

## Visual adventure branches

### The Luminous Archive [PROVISIONAL]
An underground, living archive that remembers every soul. Scenes: descending through crystal, floating memory orbs, bridges above golden light, murals of the knight's own battles, memories of lost friends, threads that stand for names and lives, coming back up at dawn. It fits the world well, though it's a visual story, not confirmed geopolitical canon.

### Valley of Forgotten Gods [PROVISIONAL]
A valley of enormous abandoned divine statues: colossal moss-covered idols, fallen god-hands lying in lakes, cracked statues full of flowers, broken colossi, faint voices, and gods asking only to dream again. Its location and history are undefined.

### The Summit Road [GAME TEXT]
A studio piece: the cursed warrior of the Old Age legend, seen from behind, walking a paved road along a terraced ridge toward a hazy spire crowned with green lightning, between colossal blades left from the war of gods, above a sea of cloud. Six captions tell the legend and loop forever (all six are in [17-studio-and-games.md](17-studio-and-games.md)); the summit never gets closer. You liked its lighting, not the rest. The legend is in [05-ancient-history.md](05-ancient-history.md). Unlike the Luminous Archive and the Valley above, this piece illustrates a legend that is your canon, so the scenery is flavor but the story is not.

- **[CANON]** (studio lore) "Gods" in the god-war era means godlike to humans, not universe creators. Mythological-tier beings fought.
- **[CANON]** (studio lore) A "dirty deceiver" was a minor godlike being that gifted the cursed sword to a king's son.
- **[CANON]** (studio lore) The eastern kingdom's system exists "to take people further from their God, so minor deities can rule over their kingdoms." So the world has a God (singular, capitalized in your notes) and minor deities who rule kingdoms, as well as the godlike beings of the god war. See [02-magic.md](02-magic.md).

### Parallel dimensions

The bible had one; the studio adds two. Keep them distinct until you say otherwise.

- **[CANON]** (this bible, [03-knights-and-orders.md](03-knights-and-orders.md)) The parallel-dimension order moved into "a parallel dimension where time is almost frozen" and returns through vessels.
- **[CANON]** (studio lore) The summit dimension: "a parallel dimension of Old magic", a near-infinite, symmetrical, gradually sloped walkable mountain under stormy magical clouds, with a colossal summit that was the site of an ancient godly battle. Time passes there: the cursed warrior has walked it for centuries or millennia. It is "empty". The rally scroll is the one way in that your notes describe.
- **[CANON]** (studio lore) The Covenant's Hunter's dimension: the creature "fights, blinks into a parallel dimension for a few seconds, then resumes, while for the knight 20 years or more may have passed. No one knows how it works." Muster calls its home "Between moments". The knights' side is in [03-knights-and-orders.md](03-knights-and-orders.md).
- **[PROPOSED FIX]** The Hunter's dimension and the order's dimension have the same time relationship (seconds inside, decades outside). They may be one place. The summit dimension is not: time runs there. Nothing in your notes links any of the three; this is only an observation.
- **[FLAVOR]** (this bible, ref 05) The sky jellyfish's "parallel realm" is a fourth candidate, already proposed as the order's dimension in P10.
