## Relic Vine

- **[CANON]** (unplaced) A magical fruit species long extinct. All that remains is an ancient living vine, like a centuries-old real grapevine, carrying an echo of the vanished fruit. (studio lore)
- **[CANON]** (unplaced) It connects to the sun-as-prison cosmology below. (studio lore)
- **[PROPOSED FIX]** It could stand in the Reach as an Elder Frontiers site (see the Atlas in [01-the-reach.md](01-the-reach.md)): a living relic older than the castles, kin to the Colossal Starblooms in value and to the Forest of Eyes in strangeness ([06-places.md](06-places.md)), and a natural Collector's find (the Collectors in [03-knights-and-orders.md](03-knights-and-orders.md)). Its echo of a vanished fruit would fit the Reach's standing mystery of magical life without ambient magic ([02-magic.md](02-magic.md)). Whether it belongs to the Reach, to the eastern kingdom or to neither is your call.

## The sun as a prison

- **[CANON]** (unplaced) A cosmology: a god sacrificed his human form to permanently trap a corrupt peer-tier entity, and the frozen result is visible daily in the sky. (studio lore)
- **[PROPOSED FIX]** Three places it could connect, and they conflict, so you must choose. (a) The god-war era of your eras ("gods" meaning godlike to humans, not universe creators; studio lore, see [05-ancient-history.md](05-ancient-history.md)): a god trapping a peer fits there as the era's last act. (b) Still Water's red sun, which "is not a sun" but an eye on a line: the prison could be what the fish's "sun" really is, or a different body in the same sky. (c) The Memory Eclipse's black sun in [09-mythic-saga.md](09-mythic-saga.md). Recommendation: (a) as the era, with (b) and (c) left open until you say which sky this is. The archived Agartha branch had "black-sun controls" ([12-archive.md](12-archive.md)); don't import that.

## The speech-delay elixir

- **[CANON]** (unplaced) A magic system built on a speech-delay elixir. Nothing more is recorded. (studio lore)
- **[PROPOSED FIX]** The Reach already runs on words being binding: the knights won their covenant with wordplay (studio lore, [working]), the fish grants wishes word for word, the stone folk learned exactly two human words. A system in which speech is delayed, and so weighed before it lands, would sit naturally beside Verdant precision magic ([02-magic.md](02-magic.md)), or it could be the eastern kingdom's craft. You need to say what the elixir does before it is placed.

- **[PROPOSED FIX]** Kabbalistic minimalism is one of the styles you kept ("good for concepts", see [17-studio-and-games.md](17-studio-and-games.md)), so this is a title screen or a studio vignette waiting to be made, perhaps in the engine's Phase 4. As lore it rhymes with Verdant magic's tiny intervention with a large result ([02-magic.md](02-magic.md)) and with the gentle garden golems ([07-creatures-and-peoples.md](07-creatures-and-peoples.md)). Don't assume the knight is the red-caped knight ([08-characters-and-stories.md](08-characters-and-stories.md)).
