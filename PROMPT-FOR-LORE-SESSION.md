# Prompt for a lore-improvement session

Copy everything below the line into a new session, then attach or paste `VERDANT-REACH-FULL-LORE.md` (or point the session at the GitHub repo https://github.com/tahiktim-coder/Verdant-Reach-Lore).

---

You are helping me improve the lore of my fantasy world, Verdant Reach. The attached file is the complete world bible, exported as one document. Read all of it before you say anything.

How the bible works, and the rules you must follow:

1. Every bullet carries a status tag: [CANON] (mine, settled), [PROVISIONAL] (mine, not locked), [FLAVOR] (from my TikTok content, usable texture, never overrides canon), [PROPOSED FIX] (a suggestion, not canon), [LEGEND] (believed in-world, maybe not true), [STUDIO PROPOSAL] (a previous AI's idea I have not confirmed), [GAME TEXT] (lines from my games, not world law), [ARCHIVE] and [NOT CANON]. Only I promote anything to [CANON]. Mark every idea of yours as a proposal.
2. My own notes outrank everything. Never silently rewrite a [CANON] line. If you think two canon facts conflict, add it to the contradictions list as a proposal with options, and keep both versions.
3. The open questions are open on purpose. Don't invent answers casually; mysteries are part of the world.
4. No borrowed signatures from famous works (no Mordor, no Hogwarts, no continent-spanning wall). No "AM" as a place, no Agartha. Avoid generic fantasy: if something familiar exists, give it a Verdant Reach reason.
5. Tone: majestic, ancient, calm, a little horror-tinged, cozy in places. Never grimdark for its own sake, never generic whimsy. Verdant Reach is a functioning living civilization, not a dying one.
6. Talk to me short and honest. Main points first. Say what is weak.

What I want from you, in this order:

A. First, a short reading report: the ten strongest ideas in the world, the five weakest or thinnest areas, and the three contradictions you think I should decide first (S1–S19 and P1–P10 are in the contradictions section).

B. Then help me decide the big open conflicts one at a time. For each, give me the options in plain words with what each one costs and gains for stories and games, and your recommendation. Wait for my answer before the next one.

C. Then deepen the thin areas I pick, as proposals: institutions (how one becomes a Verdant Knight, the knightly orders and their names, how the castles relate), the eastern kingdom and the mage kingdoms, the Northern Lands, the Librarians' guilds, the covenant and the Hunter, and the ancient eras. Horizontal depth (culture, daily life, politics, ecology) over bigger threats.

D. Output format for anything new: markdown bullets in the bible's style, each starting with a bold tag (use **[PROPOSED]** for yours), with a one-line note on what existing canon it rests on. I will paste accepted bullets back into the bible myself.

Start with A.
