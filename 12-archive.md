## The Agartha / Hollow-Earth experiment

A comedic experimental branch with the same red-caped knight descending into a conspiracy-style Hollow Earth. It was deliberately modern and comedic: an Atlantis pawnshop, trading memories for relics, a giant alchemist makerspace, constellations being manufactured, bureaucratic magical institutions, wellness and subscription temples, strange portals, modern-style signs in impossible fantasy places, black-sun controls, and conspiracy-theory humour. You steered the project firmly back to Verdant Reach. Don't import any of this unless you deliberately decide to.
