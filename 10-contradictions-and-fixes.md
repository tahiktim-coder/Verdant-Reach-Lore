# Contradictions and Fixes

**Resolved** means you approved the fix or it came from your own correction. **Proposed** means it waits for your decision. Each fix tries to keep the strongest parts of both versions. **S-items** (studio lore vs. the bible) are all Proposed; each lists what the bible says, what your studio notes say, the options, and a severity (major = changes the shape of the world; minor = wording or placement).

## Resolved

### R1. Southwestern kingdoms vs. a huge land of castles, not countries
- **Old (June 2025):** The Reach was a southwestern region of kingdoms among green valleys and alpine mountains.
- **New (about a week later, dedicated lore):** The Reach is huge, with castles but no countries.
- **Fix:** The Reach is an enormous territory with many regions. The southwestern alpine valleys are one famous heartland, probably where traditional Verdant Knight culture grew. The old "kingdoms" were **castle realms**: domains that control land, villages, armies and sometimes fleets. They look like kingdoms to outsiders but aren't nations in the continental sense.

### R2. Centuries of peace vs. huge armies, fleets and defences
- **Fix:** Peace comes from restraint, not weakness. Everyone knows what war between castles would cost. Forces exist for defence, ancient obligations, dangerous creatures, expeditions and outside threats. Military power is everywhere but culturally restrained, which fits knights who are learned and not bloodthirsty.

### R3. The Reach stays out of world politics vs. Verdant Knights in every country
- **Fix:** The Reach as a political whole rarely intervenes. Individual knights do, as scholars, envoys, mediators, monster hunters and keepers of agreements. That gives the Reach soft influence without an empire. Foreign rulers meet knights but almost never a Verdant army.

### R4. Low magic vs. lots of magical creatures
- **Fix (your correction):** The Reach is poor in ambient magic but rich in magical life. Creatures don't rely on the ambient field the way mages do. Where their magic comes from is a deliberate mystery.

### R5. Unicorns: rare vs. present
- **Fix (your correction):** Unicorns exist. Magical unicorns belong to the nobility of the Verdant Knights. They're rare to ordinary people because of social access, not because few of them exist.

### R6. "Low magic forced sophistication" vs. the original wording
- **Fix:** Use the original, sharper idea. Verdant practitioners learned exactly what magic does and where a tiny intervention has a huge result. "I understand reality well enough that I barely need to."

### R7. TikTok lore vs. dedicated lore
- **Fix (your rule):** Dedicated lore chats outrank TikTok material. TikTok ideas are flavor unless promoted. See the source hierarchy in [README.md](README.md).

## Proposed: awaiting your decision

### P1. Castle knight counts (3 to 20) vs. travelling groups of four or more
**Proposed:** No real conflict. 3 to 20 counts a castle's elite resident knights. Travelling patrols and expeditions are separate units and may include junior knights.

### P2. The grounded First Quest knight vs. the mythic red-caped knight across all ages
This is the biggest open continuity question. Options:
- **(a) Legend:** The saga is myth that grew around a real man. Later ages remembered and distorted him. This fits the Storm of Saints and the Hollow God especially well, since the whole point of those chapters is that the myth stops being the man.
- **(b) The cloak is inherited:** Different knights take up the red cloak across the ages.
- **(c) The same man, with time involved:** Dragon-egg youth, the near-frozen dimension, Hourglass Isle or the Cavern of Giants could explain a knight who lives through the ages.
- **(d) Purely symbolic:** The saga is an artistic retelling, not history.
- **(e) Reincarnation:** The same soul returns in different ages.

**Recommendation:** (a), with a possible touch of (b). It keeps the grounded knight human, fits the existing saga themes, and avoids an immortal-hero problem. Note the irony in (c): eating dragon eggs to stay young is exactly what drove the First Quest villain mad.

### P3. Two saga branches: two knights (Beasts → Depths → Titans) vs. one knight (First Light → … → Quiet Wonder)
**Proposed:** Merge them. Branch A becomes the deep prehistory between **The First Light** and **The First Kingdoms**: Age of Beasts → Abyssal Depths → Fall of Titans. The fall of the titans clears the world for the first kingdoms. Branch A already pointed toward the Age of Artifice, so this fits. The two knights can be read as legend, or as the companion pairing (red-caped knight and the blonde knight).

### P4. Memory Eclipse and Quiet Wonder order
**Proposed:** Hollow God → Memory Eclipse → Age of Quiet Wonder. See [09-mythic-saga.md](09-mythic-saga.md).

### P5. "The South" vs. "multiple Mage Kingdoms"
**Proposed:** Same region at different zoom levels. The South is the populous, magic-rich region, and the Mage Kingdoms are its states. The early "mage society starting to crack" idea becomes rivalry between those kingdoms plus Y's influence.

### P6. The ancient arcane-mech civilization vs. the mythic Age of Artifice vs. the Silent Battlefield machines
**Proposed:** One historical thread seen from three angles. The Age of Artifice is the mythic memory of the arcane-mech civilization, the Silent Battlefield is the scar from its final war, and the southern mages are those who overthrew it afterward. Optional: keep some of these deliberately unconnected for mystery.

### P7. The North's four kingdoms
**Proposed:** Keep them. Nothing in later lore contradicts them, so they can stay as the North's political structure until you replace them.

### P8. Ancient machinery immune to magic (early) vs. later lore that doesn't mention it
**Proposed:** Soften it to "ancient machinery is highly resistant to magic." This keeps the North/South tension and fits the Stone Hands, which cancel magic. It may also hint at how the old civilization was so strong, and how the mages still overthrew it.

### P9. Silly creatures vs. a serious world
**Proposed:** Not a contradiction, just placement. Silly or wholesome creatures live mostly in **Hearthpaths**, and serious horrors live in **Elder Frontiers / Forbidden Roots**. The atlas tiers handle tone.
