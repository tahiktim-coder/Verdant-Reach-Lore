# Verdant Reach — World Bible

The organized record of everything established about Verdant Reach so far, rebuilt from the ChatGPT reconstruction pasted on 2026-10-01 and the corrections made in that conversation.

## How to read this bible

Every idea is tagged with a status so lore never gets lost or quietly changed again.

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[CANON]** | Came from a dedicated lore conversation or real story development. This is the source of truth. |
| **[PROVISIONAL]** | The idea is real and yours, but its name, details or placement are not locked yet. |
| **[FLAVOR]** | Came from TikTok/visual work. It fits the world and can be used as texture, but it does not override canon. It can be promoted to canon later. |
| **[PROPOSED FIX]** | A suggested way to resolve a contradiction or gap. Not canon until you approve it. |
| **[LEGEND]** | Believed by characters in the world, but not necessarily objectively true. |
| **[ARCHIVE]** | An assistant invention you never adopted, or an abandoned branch. Kept for reference only. |
| **[NOT CANON]** | Explicitly rejected. Never use. |
| **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** | A sub-kind of PROVISIONAL. A previous Claude in the Reach Studio proposed it (marked [proposed] in `studio/lore/*.md`) and you have not confirmed it. Keep it, say it came from the studio, never present it as yours. |
| **[GAME TEXT]** | Player-facing text co-written in the studio's games: tips, lore lines, tap lines, item descriptions, captions, endings. It is the games' actual script and usable flavor, not settled world canon unless the idea under it is marked [his] in a studio lore note. Stats, tiers and counts in the games are never lore. |

A tag in a heading, or in the line that introduces a list, applies to every untagged bullet under it.

### Source hierarchy (highest wins)

1. Explicit lore discussions: magic, politics, history, institutions, regions.
2. Explicit story development: the First Quest, characters, real events.
3. Ideas that recurred across visual/TikTok work and clearly became part of the world.
4. One-off TikTok jokes, captions and prompts. These are flavor only until promoted. The idea underneath may be canon, but modern phrasing like RPG terms, "max defense" or "good boys" is not formal lore wording.
5. Assistant ideas you never accepted.

The reference images in [14-visual-references.md](14-visual-references.md) sit at level 3–4: a loose idea bank whose details can be changed freely. They are flavor, not canon.

When two real pieces of lore conflict, the goal is a fix that keeps the strongest parts of both, not deleting one. Never quietly turn provisional material into canon.

### The Reach Studio folder

`studio/` holds the Reach Studio project: the games' visuals and prototypes, the art direction, the pixel-scenes method and the plan for a tiny engine. See [17-studio-and-games.md](17-studio-and-games.md).

- `studio/lore/verdant-reach.md`, `still-water.md`, `kolobok.md`, `muster-roster.md` and `other-ideas.md` were compiled from your saved notes. They are **top-tier source documents**, equal to levels 1 and 2 above. In them **[his]** means your idea (CANON here), **[working]** means your idea still being shaped (PROVISIONAL here) and **[proposed]** means the studio's Claude proposed it and you have not confirmed it (STUDIO PROPOSAL here). Where a studio note and this bible conflict, neither side wins automatically: the conflict goes to [10-contradictions-and-fixes.md](10-contradictions-and-fixes.md).
- `studio/lore/still-water-story-map.md` is different: it was saved from a claude.ai artifact and its lines are the studio's proposed copy, so it is **[STUDIO PROPOSAL]** throughout.
- Player-facing text inside the games (tips, lore lines, tap lines, item descriptions, captions, endings) is **[GAME TEXT]**: the games' script and usable flavor, not settled canon.
- The pieces' own READMEs, code comments and roadmap are build notes, not lore. Stats, tiers, counts and growth rates in the games are placeholders and never lore.

## Core identity in one paragraph

Verdant Reach is an enormous, ancient land of castles, not countries, inside a larger medieval-fantasy world. Ambient magic there is extremely weak, yet the land is full of magical creatures. Because so little magical energy is available, its mages and knights built a precise, intellectual, low-power tradition: they outthink the world instead of overpowering it. Its castles hold knowledge older than most foreign kingdoms. Its scholar-knights travel everywhere as trusted neutral figures. Its many independent and secret orders make the Reach almost impossible for outsiders to understand or conquer. Outside it lie very different magical civilizations, and beneath everything lies a forgotten arcane-mechanical past whose machines are still running. Through all of this, people still travel, eat honey from local castles, study in libraries, raise dragons and drink tea in inns run by ghosts.

**Tone:** majestic, ancient, melancholic, dark, mysterious, cozy, adventurous, strange, and sometimes funny, but never generic grimdark and never generic whimsy. It is an incredibly old world, and ancient history should feel much larger than the current age. Many ruins are older than today's political systems. Medieval fantasy that can be light or dark. Ancient mystery rather than generic high fantasy. Relic technology that still feels like fantasy, not science fiction. Realistic politics. Many mysteries stay unexplained. The world is monumental and cozy at the same time, and neither side should erase the other.
